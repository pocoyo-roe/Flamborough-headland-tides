Why the tides here are unusual

    Local terms

    On this coast the flood runs south and the ebb runs north. Times are from high water at Bridlington.

    Zoom in to see the local names of the coves, rocks and cliffs round the headland.

    How to read this

    Each magenta ◆ is a tidal diamond, as printed on ADMIRALTY charts. Its table gives the direction the stream flows towards (set, °T) and its speed (rate, knots) for every hour from six hours before to six hours after high water at the reference port, at spring and at neap tides.

    Between diamonds the map blends the tables by distance, and between whole hours it blends them in time. The moving streaks show the result: faster water is darker and moves faster.
